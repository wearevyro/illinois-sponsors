School Network
School Partners

Illinois Sponsors works directly with high school athletic programs across the state — bringing brand partnerships that generate revenue, visibility, and community investment.

01
Revenue for Athletics

Sponsorship fees flow directly toward supporting athletic programs — covering equipment, travel, uniforms, and operational costs that school budgets often cannot fully fund on their own.

02
Zero Administrative Burden

Vyro handles all creative coordination, installation, partner management, and reporting — so athletic directors and school administrators have no operational burden from the sponsorship program.

03
Community Investment

Sponsors participate in student-focused initiatives, recognition programs, and community events that create lasting impact — extending the relationship well beyond game day signage.

04
NIL Program Support

For eligible schools and athletes, Illinois Sponsors connects student-athletes with compliant NIL partnership opportunities — building pathways to brand relationships that extend beyond high school.

05
Scholarship & Award Programs

Brand-named scholarship and recognition programs give sponsors a visible, meaningful way to invest in students — recognized at events, in school communications, and across social channels.

06
Multi-School Network Access

One sponsorship relationship can activate across multiple schools in our network simultaneously — maximizing reach across different communities, districts, and demographics throughout Illinois.
