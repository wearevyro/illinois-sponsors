Common Questions
Who is Illinois Sponsors?

A media rights and commercial partnerships company that operates the commercial business of sports and entertainment properties across Illinois — multimedia rights, corporate partnerships, and premium experiences. We are a Vyro company.

Do you only work with big properties?

No. We work across every level — pro teams and venues, college athletic departments and conferences, tournaments and events, and the brands that want to reach their audiences.

What does it cost?

Property engagements are structured as multi-year management agreements, revenue shares, or rights fees, built around the property. Brand partnerships are priced on the assets and audience. We'll walk through numbers on the first call.

How fast can we start?

We respond to every inquiry within one business day and can usually have a working proposal back within a week of the first conversation.
