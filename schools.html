Worth Knowing

Illinois opened up high school NIL.

Illinois now permits high school athletes to earn from their name, image, and likeness. That changes what a high school partnership can look like — and creates real compliance questions for schools and districts.

Illinois Sponsors keeps school and athlete deals cleanly separated: the school's commercial program stays with the institution, and anything athlete-facing is handled through Vyro's advisory side, built to protect the school's position.
