What We Do

We Build and Manage
Your Entire Commercial Business.

Illinois Sponsors is a commercial partnerships and revenue management company that helps sports organizations, universities, leagues, events, and entertainment properties maximize the value of their commercial rights. Finding sponsors is only one part of the business.

What Illinois Sponsors Does

"We'll build and manage your entire commercial business."

Imagine a university hires Illinois Sponsors. Instead of saying, "We'll find you sponsors," Illinois Sponsors says, "We'll build and manage your entire commercial business."

That could include:

01 · Corporate Partnerships

Corporate Partnerships

We build and manage official partnership programs, connecting national and regional brands to your audience through presenting rights, category partnerships, and integrated campaigns.

02 · Premium Hospitality

Premium Hospitality

Suites, clubs, and premium experiences developed and sold as year-round hospitality programs for corporate partners.

03 · Naming Rights

Naming Rights

We develop, value, and secure naming partnerships across venues, facilities, and events, structured as long-term, marquee commitments.

04 · Multimedia Rights

Multimedia Rights

We own and operate the media inventory of our properties, radio, streaming, podcasts, digital, and social, managed as one integrated media business. This is the core of the model national rights holders like Learfield built in college athletics.

05 · Licensing

Licensing

We manage licensing and merchandising programs that monetize your marks and trademarks across retail, e-commerce, and brand collaborations.

06 · Data & Insights

Data & Insights

Audience analytics, sponsorship valuation, and reporting that inform pricing, guide strategy, and prove return for every partner.

07 · Activation

Activation

Full-service activation and fulfillment that deliver on every partnership commitment and drive renewals.

08 · Commercial Strategy

Commercial Strategy

Rights valuation, category strategy, and pricing that define what your platform is worth and where its revenue can grow.

How It Works

Built to grow when you grow.

We structure long-term partnerships, not one-off engagements. Depending on the property, Illinois Sponsors operates through multi-year management agreements, revenue sharing, and rights fees, aligning our commercial success directly with yours.

The structure is built around each property. When your commercial business grows, so does ours.

Our Mission

Illinois Sponsors partners with sports, education, and entertainment organizations to develop, manage, and grow their commercial businesses, and to unlock the full value of their brands and audiences. Not another sponsorship company.
