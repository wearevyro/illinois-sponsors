What We Do

Media. Sponsorship.
Advisory.

Three connected capabilities that turn Illinois attention into measurable brand value — delivered as one integrated partnership.

01 — Media

Media

Reach an Illinois audience wherever it watches. We place brands inside the broadcasts, streams, and content that fans already follow — then extend that reach across social and digital so a single partnership works on every screen.

Media is bought on measurable delivery, not impressions on paper. Every placement is reported on reach and engagement.

  • Broadcast mentions & voiceovers
  • Streaming & livestream integration
  • Podcast sponsorship
  • Branded content series
  • Social & digital placement
  • Highlight & recap sponsorship
  • Email newsletter placement
  • Press & editorial co-branding
02 — Sponsorship

Sponsorship

Own the moments fans remember. From field and venue signage to presenting rights and on-site activation, sponsorship embeds a brand into the live experience of Illinois sports and entertainment.

Packages scale from single events to full seasons, with category exclusivity available at the presenting level.

  • Venue & field signage
  • Presenting & title rights
  • On-site activation
  • Event & tournament sponsorship
  • Season-long packages
  • Hospitality & VIP
  • Sampling & experiential
  • Category exclusivity
03 — Advisory & Services

Advisory & Services

The operating layer behind every partnership. We value inventory on real audience data, build the strategy, produce the creative, manage the activation, and report on outcomes.

Advisory is how attention becomes revenue — for the brand and the property alike.

  • Inventory valuation
  • Partnership strategy
  • Creative production
  • Media planning
  • Activation management
  • Performance reporting
  • Renewal & growth planning
  • Compliance & brand safety
