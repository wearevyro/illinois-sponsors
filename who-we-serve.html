Every levelof the game.

From professional venues to collegiate athletics to the brands that reach them, Illinois Sponsors represents and grows the commercial business of properties at every level.

Four Audiences · One Partner

Wherever you sit, we build.

01 — Pro
Teams & venues

Professional teams and venues carry the largest audiences in the market. We maximize partnership revenue and deepen fan engagement without diluting the brand — from presenting rights to premium hospitality.

02 — College
Athletic departments

Collegiate properties are professionalizing their multimedia rights. We provide the valuation, packaging, and reporting to compete for national and regional partners — sustainable revenue that funds the program, year after year.

03 — Youth
Schools & clubs

Schools, clubs, and youth programs command real local attention. We turn that into sustainable funding — handling creative, placement, and partner management so staff carry no operational burden.

04 — Brands
National & regional

Brands want authentic, measurable reach into Illinois communities. We connect them to the right properties and audiences, then prove the impact with reporting — one partner, one point of contact, inventory across every level.
