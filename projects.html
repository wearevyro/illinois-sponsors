Illinois Pro-Am Series & Community Activation

Football Season Community Campaign

Raising Cane's activated across the full Illinois high school football season — sponsoring nine home varsity games with stadium scoreboard signage, PA announcements, and post-game feeding events for players, coaches, and families. The campaign extended into youth camps and church-community feeding days, turning game-day moments into sustained neighborhood investment throughout the Chicago metro area.
