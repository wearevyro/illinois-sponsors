The Story
How Illinois Sponsors Came to Be

Illinois Sponsors was built on decades of civic and community work across the state of Illinois — earning trust at the highest levels of public life. From programming with the Obama Foundation to municipal partnerships with the office of Mayor Richard M. Daley, our team has spent years operating at the intersection of brands, institutions, and community impact in Chicago and across Illinois.

That foundation made one thing clear: the communities with the most institutional support had the most thriving programs — and the brands that invested in those communities earned something no traditional media buy could replicate. Trust. Visibility. Civic equity.

In 2026, Illinois Sponsors was acquired by Shawn Jones and Vyro Companies™ — bringing the platform under a national infrastructure company purpose-built for sports, media, and entertainment partnerships. The acquisition expanded Illinois Sponsors beyond school athletics into a full-spectrum partnership network spanning athletes, creators, live events, digital media, and community initiatives — all under one operating system.

The company operates with a focus on long-term infrastructure, strategic partnerships, and purpose-driven investment — building real value for every brand, school, athlete, and community it works with across Illinois.

Illinois Sponsors now represents a growing network of partner schools, local businesses, national brands, athletes, and creators united by a shared conviction: that investing in Illinois communities is investing in what's next — and that civic-grade execution is what separates a partnership from a transaction.
