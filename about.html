The Story
How Illinois Sponsors Came to Be

Illinois Sponsors was built on decades of community work across the state — earning trust through programming with the Obama Foundation, municipal partnerships with the office of Mayor Richard M. Daley, and years of operating at the intersection of brands, institutions, and community impact in Chicago and across Illinois.

That work made one thing clear: visibility built through genuine community engagement outlasts any media buy. The brands that show up — through sports, culture, live experiences, and real activation — earn something that no impression can manufacture. Relevance.

In 2026, Illinois Sponsors became part of Vyro Companies™ — a national infrastructure company built for sports, media, and entertainment. That move gave Illinois Sponsors the operating system, creative capacity, and network to scale its work statewide across sponsorships, activations, media, live events, and brand storytelling.

Today, Illinois Sponsors is a sports, media, entertainment, and community influence company. We help brands, businesses, organizations, and initiatives connect with real communities through strategic visibility infrastructure — built for long-term relevance and measurable impact.

From sponsorship campaigns and community activations to media placements, events, signage, and brand storytelling — we build what's next.
