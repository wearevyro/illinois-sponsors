About Illinois Sponsors

Built to Power
Partnerships.

Illinois Sponsors is a sports, media, and entertainment partnership company. We connect brands to the audiences and venues of Illinois athletics — and turn that attention into measurable return.

01
Our Mission

To connect brands with the audiences, venues, and communities of Illinois athletics — and to build partnerships that generate measurable visibility, revenue, and long-term relevance.

02
Our Vision

An Illinois where every property has the infrastructure to grow, and every brand has a credible, measurable way to reach the communities that matter most.

03
Our Values

Return over reach. Infrastructure over one-off placements. Measurement over guesswork. Partnerships built to compound, season after season.

Our Approach
How we build partnerships.

Illinois Sponsors sits between two sides of the same equation. On one side are the brands that want to reach Illinois. On the other are the properties — the teams, venues, and events — that command its attention. Our job is to connect them, price the partnership fairly, and manage it end to end.

We treat sponsorship as infrastructure, not advertising. That means building inventory that can be valued, packaging it into partnerships that fit real objectives, and reporting on outcomes so every dollar can be traced to impact.

The result is partnership that lasts. Brands get exposure they can measure. Properties get revenue they can rely on. And the communities behind Illinois sports get investment that shows up where it counts.

How We Operate
Four principles.
01
Measurable by Design
Every partnership ships with reporting. Reach, engagement, and visibility are tracked from day one.
02
Full-Service Execution
We handle strategy, creative, placement, and logistics — so partners carry zero operational burden.
03
Fair, Transparent Value
Inventory is valued on real audience data, not inflated rate cards.
04
Built for the Long Term
We structure partnerships to renew and grow, not to churn.
