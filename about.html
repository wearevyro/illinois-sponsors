Illinois Sponsors was created with a single conviction: that the spirit of community athletics is one of the most powerful forces in Illinois life — and that force deserves infrastructure, investment, and institutional support.

The Story
How Illinois Sponsors Came to Be

Illinois Sponsors was built on the back of decades of civic work across the state of Illinois — earning trust at the highest levels of public life. From community programming with the Obama Foundation to municipal partnerships with the office of Mayor Richard M. Daley, our team has spent years operating at the intersection of brands, institutions, and community impact in Chicago and across Illinois.

That foundation made one thing clear: the schools with the most community support had the most thriving athletics programs — and the brands that invested in those communities earned something no traditional media buy could replicate. Trust. Visibility. Civic equity.

In 2026, Illinois Sponsors was acquired by Shawn Jones and Vyro Companies™ — bringing the network under a national infrastructure platform purpose-built for sports, media, and entertainment partnerships. The acquisition gave Illinois Sponsors the operating system, creative capacity, and capital backing to scale its work statewide while preserving the relationships and reputation that made the original work matter.

Today, Vyro handles all creative coordination, placement logistics, and activation strategy — so sponsors can focus on showing up, not setting up. We work directly with school administrators, athletic directors, and community leaders to ensure every sponsorship is appropriate, visible, and impactful.

Illinois Sponsors now represents a growing network of partner schools, local businesses, and national brands united by a shared belief: that investing in Illinois athletics is investing in Illinois communities — and that civic-grade execution is what separates a sponsorship from a statement.
